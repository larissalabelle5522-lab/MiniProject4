Allergic

Auther:Megan Wagner Lloyd and Michelle Mee Nutter

Allergic is a comic style book about a girl who really wants a pet
dog,but she encounters a problem.The main character,Maggie, really wants this
not just because it is a puppy,but because she wants something to call her own.
Once she finally gets what she wants she finds out herself breaking into hives,
Finding she is very allergic to anything with fur!


Can Maggie fix her problem and overcome her allergies to find the perfect pet
or not; we will see!
